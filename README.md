## 🚀 Featured Engineering & Robotics Projects

### 1. 🏭 AI-Based Factory Inspection Bot
> Designed and developed an **AI-powered inspection robot** for industrial factory environments to enhance safety and operational efficiency. Integrated embedded systems with intelligent decision-making algorithms for real-time hazard detection, obstacle avoidance, and automated monitoring in restricted or high-risk zones.
* **Keywords:** `Embedded Systems` `Computer Vision / AI` `Hazard Detection` `Autonomous Navigation`

---

### 2. 🛰️ Nano MEO Environmental Monitoring Satellite
> Developed a conceptual **Nano Medium Earth Orbit (MEO) satellite system** for large-scale environmental monitoring. Implemented AI-based data analysis concepts to track atmospheric variations, pollution levels, and climate indicators through real-time telemetry and intelligent processing architecture.
* **Keywords:** `Aerospace & Satellite` `Telemetry Processing` `AI Environmental Analytics` `MEO Architecture`

---

### 3. 🐢 Mobile Robot using ROS 2 ([Turtle-Bot](https://github.com/sharveshgsarva-debug/Turtle-Bot))
> Built and programmed a **mobile robot platform using ROS 2** for autonomous navigation and control, with LiDAR and IMU sensor integration and real-time motion control for reliable movement across varied environments. Generates real-time occupancy grid maps and performs collision-free trajectory tracking.
* **Keywords:** `ROS 2` `LiDAR Sensor Fusion` `SLAM` `Autonomous Path Planning` `Python / C++`

---

### 4. 🦿 Quadruped Robot (Quadrobot)
> Designed a **four-legged walking robot** capable of stable locomotion over uneven terrain, working on mechanical design, actuator control, and gait coordination for balanced, adaptive movement.
* **Keywords:** `Quadruped Locomotion` `Actuator Control` `Kinematics & Gait Coordination` `SolidWorks CAD`

---

### 5. 🗺️ Path Planning Robot
> Developed an autonomous robot capable of planning and navigating optimal paths while avoiding obstacles in real time, utilizing path-planning algorithms and sensor-based obstacle detection.
* **Keywords:** `A* / Dijkstra Algorithms` `Real-Time Obstacle Avoidance` `Sensor Fusion` `Autonomous Drive`

---

### 6. 🔋 EV Battery Management System (BMS) ([Bidirectional-Power-IoT](https://github.com/sharveshgsarva-debug/Bidirectional-Power-IoT))
> Designed a **Battery Management System (BMS)** for electric vehicle applications to monitor battery health, state of charge (SoC), temperature, and safety parameters—improving efficiency, lifespan, and protection against overcharge or overheating. Integrated with ESP32 for live IoT telemetry.
* **Keywords:** `EV Infrastructure` `BMS Circuitry` `ESP32 IoT` `Power Electronics` `Thermal Protection`

---

### 7. 🦾 Industrial Robotics & Arm Automation
> Worked with industrial robots on automation-focused tasks, gaining hands-on experience with robotic arm operation, control systems, and integration into automated industrial workflows.
* **Keywords:** `Industrial Automation` `Robotic Arm Kinematics` `Control Systems` `Safety Interlocks`
